# Neurology-Neurodevelopment

## Overview

The Neurology-Neurodevelopment domain covers brain and spinal cord development, intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH), periventricular leukomalacia (PVL), neonatal seizures, hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy (overlapping with Asphyxia-Resuscitation), neurodevelopmental follow-up, and outcomes by gestational age. This domain carries some of the highest stakes in neonatology — neurologic injury is the primary driver of long-term disability after preterm birth and asphyxia, and board questions in this area heavily test mechanism, risk factors, and surveillance strategies.
