# Water-Salt-Renal

## Overview

The Water-Salt-Renal domain covers fluid balance and insensible water loss in the preterm infant, electrolyte homeostasis (sodium, potassium, calcium, phosphorus), renal development and function, acute kidney injury in the neonate, renal tubular disorders, and diuretic pharmacology. Fluid and electrolyte management is a core daily skill in the NICU; understanding renal development is essential for dosing medications and interpreting laboratory values at different gestational ages.
