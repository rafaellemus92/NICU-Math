# Nutrition-GI

## Overview

The Nutrition-GI domain covers neonatal nutritional requirements, enteral and parenteral nutrition strategies, breastfeeding physiology and support, gastrointestinal development, and GI disease. Key pathologies include necrotizing enterocolitis, surgical GI conditions, and metabolic bone disease of prematurity. Nutritional support is a daily NICU management priority and a board topic spanning normal requirements, TPN composition, feeding advancement, and disease-specific modifications.
