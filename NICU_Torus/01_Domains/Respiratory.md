# Respiratory

## Overview

The Respiratory domain covers the full arc of lung biology in the neonate: from the embryological events that establish airway architecture, through the biochemical preparation for air breathing, to the mechanical and vascular transitions at birth, and into the diseases that arise when any of these steps fail. It is among the highest-yield domains for neonatal boards, spanning physiology (surfactant, lung mechanics), acute pathology (RDS, air leak, PPHN), chronic disease (BPD), and structural anomalies (CDH, pulmonary hypoplasia, ACD).

## Cross-Domain Links

- [[Cardiovascular]] — [[Pulmonary Hypertension]] and [[Extrauterine Pulmonary Transition]] are fundamentally cardiopulmonary: pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR) drop at birth is driven by oxygenation, lung inflation, and loss of placental prostaglandins. See [[Pulmonary Vascular Resistance]] and [[Transitional Circulation]] in the Cardiovascular domain.
- [[Cardiovascular]] — PPHN hemodynamics: right-to-left shunting at the foramen ovale and ductus arteriosus creates differential cyanosis. See [[PPHN Hemodynamics]] and [[PDA Physiology]].
- [[Cardiovascular]] — CDH-associated pulmonary hypertension requires understanding of both vascular remodeling (Respiratory) and cardiac performance under increased afterload (Cardiovascular).
- [[Maternal-Fetal]] — Antenatal corticosteroids, oligohydramnios (CDH, pulmonary hypoplasia), polyhydramnios, PPROM, and maternal diabetes all directly shape neonatal respiratory outcomes.
- [[Pharmacology]] — Surfactant preparations, iNO delivery systems, caffeine pharmacokinetics, diuretic mechanisms, and steroid risk-benefit in BPD are detailed in the Pharmacology domain.
- [[Neurology-Neurodevelopment]] — BPD and CDH both carry significant neurodevelopmental burden; shared pathways with hypoxia and inflammation.

## Board Focus Areas

- Stages of lung development and their GA windows (embryonic, pseudoglandular, canalicular, saccular, alveolar)
- Antenatal corticosteroids: mechanism, timing, repeat dosing controversy
- Surfactant: composition, function, exogenous types, INSURE vs LISA vs intubate-and-give
- RDS: CXR pattern, pathophysiology, risk factors, management algorithm
- BPD: definition evolution (Bancalari → Jobe/Bancalari → Jensen 2019), pathogenesis (old vs new BPD), management principles
- Air leak: PIE vs pneumothorax vs pneumomediastinum vs pneumopericardium — distinguishing features and urgency
- PPHN: etiologies, pre/post-ductal saturation differential, echo findings, iNO indications, ECMO threshold
- CDH: Bochdalek vs Morgagni, survival predictors (lung-to-head ratio, liver herniation), delayed repair rationale, ECMO use
- Pulmonary hypoplasia: causes (space, fluid, movement), distinguishing from ACD
- Alveolar capillary dysplasia: FOXF1, malalignment of veins, lung biopsy diagnosis, prognosis
- TTN: ENaC molecular switch, C/S risk, furosemide NOT helpful, betamethasone dual effect
- MAS: 4 mechanisms (obstruction, pneumonitis, inactivation, PPHN), no routine suctioning (2015 NRP), surfactant works
- Apnea of prematurity: caffeine (CAP trial — reduces BPD + improves neuro), mixed most common type
- Non-invasive ventilation: SUPPORT trial (CPAP = intubation), LISA protocol, nasal septal trauma
- High-frequency ventilation: HFJV for air leaks (passive exhalation), HFOV for diffuse disease, frequency-Vt paradox
