# Hematology-Bilirubin

## Overview

The Hematology-Bilirubin domain covers fetal and neonatal hematopoiesis, red cell physiology, anemia of prematurity, polycythemia, alloimmune and autoimmune thrombocytopenia, neonatal coagulopathy, bilirubin metabolism and transport, physiologic and pathologic jaundice, phototherapy mechanisms and thresholds, and exchange transfusion. Jaundice is among the most common NICU problems and a major board topic; hematologic emergencies (thrombocytopenia, DIC, hemolytic disease) carry high morbidity.
