# Pharmacology

## Overview

The Pharmacology domain covers the principles of neonatal pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics, drug metabolism across gestational and postnatal ages, and the mechanisms, indications, contraindications, dosing, monitoring, and adverse effects of common NICU medications. It functions as both a standalone domain and a cross-domain reference layer — nearly every clinical domain relies on pharmacology for therapeutic details. High-yield board areas include therapeutic drug monitoring (gentamicin, vancomycin, caffeine, digoxin), prostaglandin pharmacology, and the specific toxicity profiles of drugs used in vulnerable neonatal populations.
