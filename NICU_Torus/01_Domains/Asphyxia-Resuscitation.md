# Asphyxia-Resuscitation

## Overview

The Asphyxia-Resuscitation domain covers intrapartum asphyxia, the NRP algorithm for delivery room stabilization and resuscitation, hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy (HIE), therapeutic hypothermia, and post-resuscitation care. It bridges the maternal-fetal and neonatal environments and is among the highest-yield board topics, requiring mastery of the NRP decision tree, the pathophysiology of hypoxic-ischemic injury, and the evidence base for cooling.
