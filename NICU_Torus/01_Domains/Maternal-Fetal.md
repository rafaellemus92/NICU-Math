# Maternal-Fetal

## Overview

The Maternal-Fetal domain covers the interface between maternal physiology, placental function, and fetal development. It encompasses uteroplacental circulation, fetal surveillance, maternal conditions that affect neonatal outcomes, fetal growth, amniotic fluid dynamics, and medications used in pregnancy. This domain provides essential context for nearly every other NICU domain — gestational age, growth trajectory, placental function, and maternal exposures shape neonatal phenotype across all organ systems.
