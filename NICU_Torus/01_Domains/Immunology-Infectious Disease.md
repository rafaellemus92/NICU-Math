# Immunology-Infectious Disease

## Overview

The Immunology-Infectious Disease domain covers the neonatal immune system, the epidemiology and pathophysiology of early- and late-onset sepsis, congenital infections (TORCH), neonatal meningitis, fungal infections, antibiotic and antifungal stewardship, and vaccination in the NICU. Infection is a leading cause of NICU morbidity and mortality; understanding host defenses, organism-specific features, and appropriate diagnostic and treatment approaches is essential for both clinical practice and board exams.
