# Endocrine-Metabolic-Thermal

## Overview

The Endocrine-Metabolic-Thermal domain covers glucose homeostasis and hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, thyroid function and congenital hypothyroidism, adrenal disorders, temperature regulation and hypothermia risk, and inborn errors of metabolism. These topics span the physiology of metabolic transition at birth through acute metabolic emergencies. Newborn screening intersects this domain significantly, and many entities here have neurodevelopmental stakes that elevate board yield.
