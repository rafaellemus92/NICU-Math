# Genetics-Dysmorphism

## Overview

The Genetics-Dysmorphism domain covers chromosomal disorders, single-gene diseases, copy number variants, and the dysmorphology approach to the newborn with anomalies. It includes the NICU genetic workup (karyotype, microarray, exome sequencing), common syndromes encountered in neonatal intensive care, and the intersection of genomics with prognosis and family counseling. Genetic context underpins disease associations across virtually every other domain.
