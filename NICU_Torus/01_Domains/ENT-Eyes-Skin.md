# ENT-Eyes-Skin

## Overview

The ENT-Eyes-Skin domain covers retinopathy of prematurity (ROP), neonatal hearing screening and congenital hearing loss, congenital eye disorders (cataracts, glaucoma, coloboma), skin development and the preterm skin barrier, wound care and skin injury prevention, and ENT anomalies (choanal atresia, cleft lip/palate, Pierre Robin sequence, laryngomalacia). Though individually narrower than other domains, ROP and hearing loss are among the most board-tested topics due to their defined screening and treatment protocols.
