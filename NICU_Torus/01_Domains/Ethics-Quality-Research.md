# Ethics-Quality-Research

## Overview

The Ethics-Quality-Research domain covers the ethical framework for periviable decision-making, goals of care conversations, palliative and comfort care, quality improvement methodology in the NICU, research ethics in vulnerable populations, evidence appraisal, and biostatistics for boards. While distinct from clinical science domains, this domain is directly tested on neonatal boards and increasingly central to fellowship training. Periviability thresholds, shared decision-making frameworks, and QI methodology (PDSA cycles, run charts, statistical process control) are the highest-yield areas.
