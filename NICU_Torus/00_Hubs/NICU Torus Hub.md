# NICU Torus Hub

> *The central navigational node for the NICU Torus knowledge vault. All 14 domain hubs, meta-axes, and structural frameworks radiate from here.*

---

## Oyster Rings Overview

The NICU Torus is organized as a set of concentric rings, each representing a different depth of clinical and conceptual engagement. Moving outward from the center means moving from foundational biology toward complex clinical application.

| Ring | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Ring 1 | Foundation | Embryology, developmental biology, normal physiology, genetics scaffolding. The "why it is built this way" layer. |
| Ring 2 | Physiology | Transitional and postnatal physiology, organ system function, homeostatic mechanisms. The "how it normally works" layer. |
| Ring 3 | Pathophysiology | Mechanisms of disease, organ dysfunction, failure patterns. The "how it breaks" layer. |
| Ring 4 | Clinical | Phenotypes, diagnosis, differential diagnosis, monitoring, interpretation. The "what you see and find" layer. |
| Ring 5 | Management | Stabilization, therapeutics, pharmacology, escalation, outcomes, QI, ethics. The "what you do" layer. |

---

## Meta-Axes

Meta-axes cut across all domains and rings, providing additional dimensions for organizing knowledge:

- **Gestational Axis** — Gestational age at the time the topic is most relevant: extremely preterm (22–27w), very preterm (28–31w), moderate/late preterm (32–36w), term (37–42w), post-term (>42w).
- **Phase Axis** — Clinical phase when the topic is most relevant: fetal, delivery room / resuscitation, early postnatal (0–72h), NICU course, transition / discharge, follow-up / neurodevelopment.
- **Maternal-Placental Axis** — Degree to which maternal or placental factors drive the neonatal presentation.
- **Systems Axis** — Primary organ system involved; used to map cross-domain overlap.
- **Board Density Axis** — Estimated high-yield board weight: foundational, high-yield, edge/pearl.

## Domain Hubs

### 1. [[Maternal-Fetal]]
Placental physiology, uteroplacental circulation, fetal well-being surveillance, maternal conditions affecting the fetus, IUGR, hydrops, medications in pregnancy.

### 2. [[Asphyxia-Resuscitation]]
Intrapartum asphyxia, NRP algorithm, delivery room stabilization, HIE, therapeutic hypothermia, post-resuscitation care.

### 3. [[Cardiovascular]]
Fetal and transitional circulation, cardiac output determinants, congenital heart disease, hemodynamic monitoring, vasoactive pharmacology, PPHN.

### 4. [[Respiratory]]
Lung development, surfactant biology, respiratory mechanics, RDS, BPD, air leak syndromes, pulmonary hypertension, CDH, pulmonary hypoplasia.

### 5. [[Genetics-Dysmorphism]]
Chromosomal disorders, single-gene disorders, dysmorphology approach, NICU genetic workup, common syndromes, genomic testing.

### 6. [[Nutrition-GI]]
Nutritional requirements, enteral and parenteral nutrition, breastfeeding, NEC, intestinal disorders, osteopenia of prematurity, GI development.

### 7. [[Water-Salt-Renal]]
Fluid balance, electrolyte disorders, renal development, acute kidney injury, renal tubular disorders, diuretic pharmacology.

### 8. [[Endocrine-Metabolic-Thermal]]
Glucose homeostasis, hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, thyroid disorders, adrenal disorders, temperature regulation, inborn errors of metabolism.

### 9. [[Immunology-Infectious Disease]]
Neonatal immune system, early- and late-onset sepsis, congenital infections (TORCH), meningitis, antifungal and antibiotic stewardship, vaccination.

### 10. [[Hematology-Bilirubin]]
Fetal and neonatal hematopoiesis, anemia, polycythemia, thrombocytopenia, coagulopathy, bilirubin metabolism, jaundice, phototherapy, exchange transfusion.

### 11. [[Neurology-Neurodevelopment]]

### 12. [[ENT-Eyes-Skin]]
Retinopathy of prematurity, hearing screening, congenital eye disorders, skin development, wound care, ENT anomalies.

### 13. [[Pharmacology]]
Pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics in neonates, drug metabolism, common NICU medications, therapeutic drug monitoring, drug interactions.

### 14. [[Ethics-Quality-Research]]
