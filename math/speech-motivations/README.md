# Breath, fricatives and vowels: motivations only

The author's [letters/words.md at the exact source commit](https://github.com/rafaellemus92/NICU-Math/blob/77916bb51ea88ea6e1b2cddd2cd6e9309993383f/letters/words.md)
uses breath, noisy fricatives (/s/, /sh/, /f/), and nearly periodic vowels or /m/
as motivation for thinking about oscillation, noise, phase and harmonics.
These are **analogies and research motivations**, not evidence connecting speech
to the periodic-cube Taylor jet or the Shared Air model.

There is no vocal-tract geometry, acoustic radiation model, speech solver,
recording dataset or clinical validation here. A finite-time physiological
transition is not a PDE singularity. Testing a speech hypothesis would require
its own equations, boundary conditions, units, data provenance and falsification
criteria, outside this import.

importing the essay's unrelated clinical/cosmological assertions, personal
correspondence context and unverified publication-status claims. Its source
citations are not revalidated by this link.
